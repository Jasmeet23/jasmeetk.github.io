About

Dr. Jasmeet Kaur is a Research Associate at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi (IIIT-Delhi), where she works at the intersection of human-computer interaction, health informatics, usable privacy, and public health.

Her research examines how digital technologies can support family planning, maternal health, and sexual and reproductive health needs in low-resource and low-literacy settings, with a focus on feminist HCI, value-sensitive design, and responsible AI.

She completed her Ph.D. at IIIT-Delhi on supporting the family planning needs of pregnant and postpartum women through smartphone-based solutions in rural Northern India and received the institute's Doctoral Dissertation Award.

Research

Research interests include Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), User Experience Research, Health Informatics, Responsible AI, Usable Privacy, Public Health, Marginalized Populations, Global Development, and ICT4D.

Usable Privacy & Responsible AI

Evaluation of AI-driven interventions through socio-cultural and value-sensitive lenses, especially in sensitive health and privacy contexts and for marginalized populations.

Reproductive Health & mHealth

Design, deployment, and evaluation of chatbots and digital peer support interventions for family planning, maternal health, and sexual and reproductive health in rural India.

Technology & Care Infrastructures

Understanding how social settings, household structures, and community dynamics shape engagement with digital health tools, and how technology can scaffold care and support.

Selected Projects

  • Chatbot for Sexual and Reproductive Health in Low-resource Settings in India (2025).
    Deployed an LLM-based WhatsApp chatbot to support postpartum women's concerns around sexual and reproductive health in low-resource settings.
  • Digital Peer Support Group for Family Planning in Low-resource Settings in India (2023).
    Conducted a three-week WhatsApp-based field study with 30 participants to examine how technology-based peer support addresses family planning needs among rural Indian women.
  • Chatbot for COVID-19 Vaccine Hesitancy in Low-resource Settings in India (2022).
    Developed, deployed, and evaluated a rule-based WhatsApp chatbot to address COVID-19 vaccine hesitancy among pregnant and breastfeeding women; analyzed engagement of over 800 users.
  • Chatbot for Breastfeeding Mothers in Low-resource Settings in India (2018).
    Designed a Hindi-language chatbot to support breastfeeding mothers by addressing breastfeeding-related queries via a mobile application interface.
