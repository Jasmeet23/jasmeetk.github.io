About

Dr. Jasmeet Kaur is a Research Associate at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi (IIIT-Delhi), where she works at the intersection of human-computer interaction, health informatics, usable privacy, and public health.

Her research examines how digital technologies can support family planning, maternal health, and sexual and reproductive health needs in low-resource and low-literacy settings, with a focus on feminist HCI, value-sensitive design, and responsible AI.

She completed her Ph.D. at IIIT-Delhi on supporting the family planning needs of pregnant and postpartum women through smartphone-based solutions in rural Northern India and received the institute's Doctoral Dissertation Award.
